Real Strategic Review · Richcrest AI's own campaign · Confidence: MEDIUM
Position

For ecommerce brands who can't produce creative fast enough to keep testing what converts, unlike AI ad generators that skip straight to output, Richcrest argues the strategy first, then directs the AI to execute it, so every creative choice has a stated reason behind it.

Why it survives

It names a bottleneck this buyer really has. It also sets itself against the option they've probably already tried, self-serve AI generators, and not traditional production, which they've likely priced out already. The main emotion is trust, because the real question is whether they can defend this spend and this vendor.

The risk

The position is argued, not proven. Confidence only moves to HIGH once there's a produced example this exact buyer would recognize as built for them.

Rejected runner-up

"A repeatable creative department." We rejected it because it implies team capacity we don't have yet, and a sharp buyer would ask about that.

A Sample For A Product Brand

The argument, then the ads it produced.

Sample review, not a client. We wrote it for two demo products, a pair of headphones and a sneaker, to show what a review and the ads that come out of it look like for a product brand.

Concept 1: Headphones

Premium before price. One spotlight, no price on screen, so the product carries the frame.

The risk

If the product can't carry the ad alone, it ends up reading like a spec sheet.

Concept 2: Sneaker review

Trust, built through a first-person, unpolished reaction, talking straight to camera.

The risk

Too much studio polish makes the review feel staged, and that kills the trust it was meant to build.
