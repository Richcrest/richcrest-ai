Common Questions

The things you are probably thinking right now.

We have heard every objection. Here are honest answers to the ones that come up most.

AI video looks fake or cheap. Won't mine too?
+
That happens when someone writes a prompt straight into an AI tool with no direction behind it. We do not do that. Every campaign starts with a creative brief: emotion, story framework, camera language, lighting, and motion, all decided before anything is generated. The AI executes a director's decision, not a guess. That is the difference between a commercial and a novelty clip.
I don't have footage, actors, or a film crew.
+
You do not need any of that. No filming required on your end. We produce the Hero Commercial and UGC review from your product details, brand direction, and a creative brief. If you do have real product photos or footage, we can incorporate them, but it is not a requirement to get a finished campaign.

+
Our Single Campaign starts at $397 and includes a Hero Commercial, a UGC review, and six more deliverables. What if I don't like the campaign?
+
We revise until it's right. Every campaign. If the direction doesn't match your brand or the tone feels off, flag it and we rework it. We also start with a free Strategic Review before you pay anything, so you see the reasoning before you commit to production.
Do I see the strategy before you make anything?
+
Yes. That's the point. Before production, you get a Strategic Review of your brand: the position we'd bet on, the emotion and story behind it, the risk, what we don't know yet, and the option we rejected. You approve the reasoning, then we produce.
How do I know this will actually convert?
+
We cannot guarantee sales because we don't control the checkout page or the offer itself. What we can guarantee is that the campaign is directed to build the specific emotion your category needs, premium feel for product brands, trust for UGC reviews, and we show you that reasoning in writing before production. That is the leverage a real commercial gives you that a phone photo cannot.
